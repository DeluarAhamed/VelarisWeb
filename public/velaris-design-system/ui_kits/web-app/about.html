We build the systems that help small businesses grow

Velaris Web designs, builds and runs websites, custom software and AI automation for service businesses, on monthly plans with no upfront cost and no contract.

Why we started

Too many business owners were paying thousands for beautiful websites that generated nothing, then paying again every time something needed to change. They didn't want a prettier website. They wanted more leads, customers who come back and fewer headaches.

What we do differently

We start with one question: what problem does your customer wake up thinking about? Then we build the tool that solves it, whether that's a faster website, an online booking and rebooking system, a CRM, an AI chatbot or a dashboard that shows what your website earns.

How we can do it for less

AI-assisted development lets us build custom tools that used to cost $50,000–$100,000 in days instead of months. So instead of a big upfront bill, we charge one monthly fee and keep improving your system as your business grows.
