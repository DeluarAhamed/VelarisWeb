The questions business owners ask most before getting a website. Can't find yours? Message us and we'll reply fast.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a website cost per month?

Our monthly website plans start at $199/month for Starter, $399/month for Growth and from $699/month for Scale. Each plan includes custom design, hosting, security, updates and SEO, with no upfront cost and no setup fee.

Is a monthly website subscription worth it?

For most small businesses, yes. Instead of paying $5,000–$15,000 upfront and then paying extra for hosting, fixes and changes, you get a professionally designed site that is hosted, secured, updated and improved every month for one predictable fee.

What is included in a monthly website plan?

Every plan includes a custom-designed responsive website, domain connection, hosting, SSL, backups and security monitoring, a professional business email, SEO setup, analytics, local listings and monthly content updates. Higher plans add blog posts, Google Business Profile management, booking systems, full e-commerce and AI automation.

Do I own my website if I pay monthly?

You own your domain, your content, your images and your brand. If you ever cancel, we hand over your content and help you move your domain. There is no lock-in.

What happens if I cancel my website subscription?

Plans are month-to-month with 30 days' notice. When you cancel, your site stays live until the end of the notice period and we hand over your content and domain so you can move it elsewhere.

How long does it take to build a website?

You'll see a first preview of your homepage within 5 business days of your kickoff call. Most small business websites launch within 2–3 weeks. Larger custom or e-commerce builds get a clear timeline at kickoff.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built with technical SEO, fast load times, metadata and Google Search Console set up, and listed on Google Maps and Apple Maps. Growth and Scale plans add on-page SEO, monthly blog content and Google Business Profile optimisation to help you rank for local searches. No one can honestly guarantee a #1 ranking, but we build for it.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We rebuild your current site on a better design and keep your important pages, URLs and redirects in place so you don't lose the Google rankings you already have.

Do you build e-commerce websites?

Yes. Starter includes a simple shop for a small product range. Growth and Scale include fully customised online stores with secure checkout, product management and payment integration.

Do I get a professional business email?

Yes. Every plan includes professional email on your own domain, such as hello@yourbusiness.com: 1 inbox on Starter, 3 on Growth and 5 on Scale.

Can I make changes to my website after it launches?

Yes. Send us a message on WhatsApp or email and we make the change for you. Starter includes 10 content updates a month; Growth and Scale include unlimited content updates.

Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in the US, UK, Europe and worldwide, with communication over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
