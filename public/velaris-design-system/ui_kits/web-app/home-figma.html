FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. You can cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

How fast will my website be ready?

You'll see a first preview of your website within 5 business days of your kickoff call. We refine it together, then launch.

Do I own my content and domain?

Yes. Your domain, copy, images and brand assets are yours. If you ever leave, we hand them over.

What counts as a content update?

Text changes, image swaps, new sections, new pages within your plan, and small layout adjustments. Send a message and we handle it.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at the start of any billing month. We add the extra features to your existing site, no rebuild needed.

Can I pay for a one-off project instead?

Yes. If you'd rather own a site outright, we still build one-off projects. Book a call and we'll quote it.
