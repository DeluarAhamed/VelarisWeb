FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Is there a setup fee?

No. You pay the monthly plan price from your first month. Scale plans are scoped on a call and may include custom work, which we always quote before starting.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. You can cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

What if I need more pages later?

Just ask. We add pages within your plan's allowance, or move you up a plan if you need more.

Do I own my content and domain?

Yes. Your domain, copy, images and brand assets are yours. If you ever leave, we hand them over.

What does "unlimited content updates" mean?

On Growth and Scale you can request text changes, image swaps, new sections or layout tweaks as often as you need. Larger new features are scoped with you first.

How fast will my website be ready?

Your first preview is ready within 5 business days of the kickoff call. Most sites launch within 2–3 weeks.
