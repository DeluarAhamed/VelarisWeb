FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Still deciding? Message us and we'll help you pick the right plan in a few minutes.

Ask on WhatsApp
Is there a setup fee?

No. You pay the monthly plan price from your first month. Scale plans are scoped on a call and any custom work is always quoted before we start.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. You can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.

Why pay monthly instead of a one-time website fee?

A one-time build usually costs $5,000–$15,000 and then hosting, security, fixes and changes are billed separately. A monthly plan spreads the cost, includes all of that, and keeps your site improving instead of going stale.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can change plans at the start of any billing month. We add the extra features to your existing site, with no rebuild and no downtime.

What does unlimited content updates mean?

On Growth and Scale you can request text changes, image swaps, new sections or layout tweaks as often as you need. Larger new features, such as a new booking system, are scoped with you first.

Is hosting and a domain included?

Hosting, SSL, daily backups and security monitoring are included in every plan, and we connect your domain for you. If you don't have a domain yet, we help you register one in your name.

How do I pay?

Plans are billed monthly in US dollars by card or bank transfer. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send everything you need to get started.

Can I buy a website outright instead?

Yes. If you'd rather own a site outright with a single payment, we also build one-off projects, scoped and quoted after a free call.
